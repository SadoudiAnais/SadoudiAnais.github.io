## About me

Since September 2026, I have been a Master 2 student at [Université d'Évry Paris-Saclay](https://www.univ-evry.fr/), where I also completed my M1 in Networks and Systems (2026) and my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, System and Network Administration track (2025).
Before that, I studied at the [University of Béjaïa](https://www.univ-bejaia.dz/) in Algeria.

From May to September 2026, I did a research internship at the [IBISC laboratory](https://www.ibisc.univ-evry.fr/).
I first worked on the ARDA web platform with Dr. Guillaume Postic, then joined [LLMThings](https://llmthings.github.io/demo/) with Dr. Massinissa Hamidi, a platform where language agents orchestrate IoT devices over MQTT and MCP.
Our paper, co-authored with Huyen-Trang Le, received the [Best Presentation Award](https://www.ibisc.univ-evry.fr/anais-sadoudi-et-huyen-trang-le-recoivent-le-best-presentation-award-a-la-jdse-2026-english-version-below/) at [JDSE 2026](https://jdse2026.github.io/program/).
I am now continuing this line of work in my M2 R&D project, *LLM-Based Multi-Agent Collaboration for IoT Orchestration*.

I also took part in the [D4Gen Hackathon 2026](https://genopole.agorize.com/en/challenges/d4gen-hackathon-2026) with the HOPPERS team, where I handled the backend and the cloud infrastructure.
On the side, I build open-source projects on LLM agents for network operations and secure IoT, which you can find on the [Projects]({{ '/software' | relative_url }}) page and on [GitHub]({{ site.links.github }}).

I speak French, English and Kabyle.
